# Spore Hollow

A calm, pixel-art mushroom grove with no objective. The clearing keeps growing on the real clock while you're away; something is dancing past the cedars.

**Play:** https://mproctr.github.io/spore-hollow/

## On Android

Open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It runs fullscreen and works offline after the first visit.

## What's in the grove

- Seven species that sprout, fruit, spore and rot on their own cycles
- Tap a mushroom in its prime to take a spore print; scatter it on the moss (or wood) or let the wind have it
- Scattered spores spread unseen for a day or two, then flush on and off for days, more after rain
- The wind seeds new colonies on its own; some species only ever arrive that way
- Rain comes and goes in spells, wetter in autumn
- Shadow folk, an owl, a pond frog, fireflies, and a party you're never quite invited to
- Ambient sound and a muffled beat that swells and fades
- Daylight follows the real sun over Vernon, BC, with sunset colours and stars after dark
- Shooting stars, more of them during real meteor showers; tap one to make a wish
- A field journal that fills in as you notice things, with a spore print collection

Everything is one file (`index.html`): canvas pixel art and Web Audio, no assets or build step. Progress is saved in the browser's local storage. To use another location, change `SITE` (latitude/longitude) near the top of the script.
